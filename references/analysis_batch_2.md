Slammed tarmac rally stance over 18-inch gold forged 6-spoke rally wheels wrapped in low-profile Pirelli rubber.

### 2. Livery Archetype & Cultural School
- **Archetype:** FIA World Rally Championship (WRC) Golden Era / Prodrive Subaru World Rally Team.
- **Cultural School:** 1990s Japanese Factory AWD Domination (Colin McRae / Nicky Grist tribute). The defining blue-and-gold visual benchmark that established Subaru's international brand equity.

### 3. Exact Color Breakdown
- **Dominant (60%):** Sonic Blue Mica (`#1845A8`) — deep, high-saturation electric competition rally blue.
- **Secondary (30%):** Fluorescent Competition Yellow (`#E5F329`) — the iconic State Express 555 crescent swoosh and crescent numerals.
- **Accent (10%):** STi Cherry Blossom Pink (`#FF007F`) on STi logo badges and typographic accents; Pure Alpine White (`#FFFFFF`) on rally door number roundel box.

### 4. Shape Grammar & Flow Line Synchronization
- **Arch-Concentric Swoosh:** The primary yellow crescent graphic hugs the swollen rear blister arch, curving organically along the quarter panel contour to emphasize the widebody shoulder line.
- **Hood Aerodynamic Vector:** The yellow hood crescent wraps tightly around the central aluminum intake scoop, directing the viewer's eye along the engine bay's cooling airflow path.
- **Horizontal Stability:** The lower rocker panel remains monochromatic blue with subtle white technical decals, grounding the car’s perceived center of gravity.

### 5. Sponsor Placement & Typography
- **Hero Billboard:** "555" (State Express 555) abstract crescent logo spanning the rear quarter panel and central hood.
- **Sanctioning Door Box:** Stark white rectangular rally competition box housing black bold condensed numeral `#1`.
- **Technical Sponsors:** "IRELLI" (Pirelli) wordmarks anchored at the outer corners of the front bumper and front fenders.
- **Windshield Brow:** Clean white "SUBARU" block capitals on blue header tint band.

### 6. Unique Motifs & Cultural Cues
- **Tobacco Censorship Crescent:** Abstract circular crescent moon motif, historically evolved to comply with European tobacco advertising bans while remaining instantly recognizable.
- **Cherry Blossom Heritage:** The "STi" logotype in traditional Sakura Cherry Blossom Pink (`#FF007F`), embodying Japanese artisanal motorsport dedication.
- **Poster Editorial Typography:** Colin McRae commemorative typographic header with blue-and-white checkered rally ribbons.

### 7. Actionable Design Rule
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Radial Flare Alignment Rule:** When placing primary sponsor graphics near flared widebody arches, force the graphic’s outer curvature to match the exact radius of the wheel arch crown. This prevents the graphic from feeling like a flat sticker slapped onto a curved 3D surface.

- **Aero & Stance:** Akira Nakai signature widebody build: bolted overfenders with visible hardware, huge "banana" rear wing with yellow uprights and black endplates, front bumper with peach canard accents, slammed stance on mismatched wheels (solid white turbofan aerodisc up front, black deep-dish mesh with stepped chrome lip at the rear), Toyo Tires.

### 2. Livery Archetype & Cultural School
- **Archetype:** Character-Themed Pop Culture / Western Comic Tribute ("Teen Titans GO! - Robin" Livery by @dp_artworks).
- **Cultural School:** Merges Japanese RWB tuner culture with Western animation hero character design.

### 3. Exact Color Breakdown
- **Dominant (60%):** Robin Emerald / Toxic Green (`#14B968`) — body shell, roof, front and rear widebody fenders.
- **Secondary (30%):** Robin Tunic Crimson Red (`#DD1F3D`) — solid red door insert panel.
- **Accent (10%):** Robin Utility Belt Yellow (`#FAD22D`) on wing uprights, side mirrors, and rocker sill extensions; Peach Flesh Tone (`#F4B79D`) on front chin lip; Pure Black (`#111111`) on bumper seals.

### 4. Shape Grammar & Flow Line Synchronization
- **Panel-Defined Anatomy:** The car's physical panels directly mirror Robin's superhero costume:
  - *Green Fenders & Roof:* Robin's green sleeves, mask, and leggings.
  - *Red Door Panel:* Robin's red chest tunic.
  - *Yellow Rocker Sills & Wing Uprights:* Robin's utility belt and yellow cape lining.
  - *Peach Chin Lip:* Robin's exposed skin tone chin.

### 5. Sponsor Placement & Typography
- **Hero Insignia:** Robin's iconic yellow "R" emblem inside a black oval centered on the crimson door.
- **Rocker Sill Typography:** Clean white/black sans-serif "XSMAG" and "TOYO TIRES" along the yellow rocker panel extension.
- **Palette Swatch Bar:** Crisp 5-block color swatch bar rendered beneath the car profile (`Green / Red / Peach / Black / Yellow`).

### 6. Unique Motifs & Cultural Cues
- **Chibi Character Illustration:** Standalone illustration of Robin in his Teen Titans GO! animated style standing heroically beside the vehicle.
- **Mismatched Wheel Styling:** Solid white turbofan front wheel paired with dark deep mesh rear wheel.

### 7. Actionable Design Rule
> [!TIP]
> **The Character Anatomical Mapping Rule:** When designing a character-tribute livery, do not simply paste the character's face onto the car; instead, map the character's costume color zones directly onto the vehicle’s anatomical panels (e.g., doors = chest tunic, rocker sills = belt, roof/fenders = cape/sleeves).

### 3. The 10 Master Axioms Distilled from Batch 2

1. **The Radial Flare Alignment Law:** Never place flat text over a widebody fender; curve typography concentrically along the flare's circumference (Subaru 22B #22).
2. **The Dark Keyline Isolation Rule:** Always insert a dark neutral pinstripe (Navy/Black) between high-saturation warm and pastel cool hues to prevent chromatic aberration (Gulf GT40 #28).
3. **The Buffer Stitch Principle:** When clashing complementary primary hues touch, insert an illustrative or graphic spacer (dashed white stitching) to convert visual tension into high-fashion identity (Mazda 787B #27).
4. **The Monochromatic Dominance Law:** A singular, ultra-saturated base coat paired with bold white block typography commands greater authority on muscular silhouettes than busy multi-tone vinyl wraps (Calsonic R32 #24).
5. **The Architectural Billboard Scale:** Scaling sponsor typography past 80% of the side flank transforms text into the car's primary graphic architecture (Hasemi 190E #31).
6. **The Drop-Shadow Camo Buffer:** When overlaying complex splinter camouflage with primary Kanji or sponsor logos, apply a heavy contrasting contour stroke to isolate the characters (Liberty Walk GT-R #32).
7. **The Heritage Subversion Principle:** Taking a legendary, pristine factory livery and distressing it with exposed welds, hazard stripes, and raw overfenders creates an irresistible outlaw narrative (Godzilla R32 #33).
8. **The Beltline Two-Tone Rule:** Utilize existing factory body side moldings and rub-strips as natural boundaries for two-tone color blocking (Mazda RX-7 FC3S #36).
9. **The Framed Panel Fine-Art Inset:** Keep complex, multi-layered artwork (such as Edo woodblock prints) confined strictly to a single architectural panel (the door skin) to avoid awkward seam fracturing (Forever Broke Onevia #37).
10. **The Character Anatomical Mapping Rule:** In character-tribute liveries, map the character's costume color blocks directly onto the car’s anatomical panels rather than pasting character faces across body creases (Robin RWB #41, Piccolo Aventador #42).
