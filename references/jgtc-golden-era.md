# The JGTC Golden Era (1993–2004) Livery Masterclass

The All Japan Grand Touring Car Championship (JGTC, later Super GT) represents the zenith of Japanese motorsport livery engineering. Cars in the GT500 and GT300 classes were not simply fast—they were mobile kinetic sculptures representing the industrial pride of Japan's automotive giants (Nissan, Toyota, Honda, Subaru, Mazda) and their corporate titans.

---

## 1. Legendary Livery Breakdowns & Color Signatures

### Calsonic Nissan Skyline GT-R (R32, R33, R34)
- **Concept:** Uncompromising monochrome velocity. The entire car is bathed in a piercing, proprietary electric cobalt blue with stark white typographic hits.
- **Palette:**
  - Calsonic Blue: `#0055A5` (RGB: 0, 85, 165 | CMYK: 100, 65, 0, 0)
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Accent Yellow (Headlights/Foglights): `#FFD200`
- **Key Graphics:**
  - Massive "CALSONIC" sans-serif bold typography along the door base and hood billboard.
  - White aerodynamic Impul turbofans / multi-spoke wheels with black or red central locknuts.
  - Number 12 roundel.
  - Bridgestone Potenza tire lettering.

### Castrol TOM'S Toyota Supra (A80)
- **Concept:** Organic kinetic ribbons. Interlocking curved red and emerald green bands sweeping over front fenders, doors, and rear haunches, framed by pure white bodywork.
- **Palette:**
  - Castrol Racing Green: `#008751` (RGB: 0, 135, 81)
  - Castrol Crimson Red: `#DE0029` (RGB: 222, 0, 41)
  - Base Pearl White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Technical Black: `#111111`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - Arcs begin low at the front bumper splitter, flow up over the front wheel arch flare, dive through the door waistline, and flare upward onto the rear wing uprights.
  - Crucial rule: Red and green sections NEVER touch directly; they are separated by negative white space to prevent color vibration.
  - Green BBS or OZ multi-spoke wheels, Dunlop tire stencils, number 36.

### Takata Dome Honda NSX (GT500)
- **Concept:** Precision emerald harness. Inspired by Takata's world-famous green racing harnesses.
- **Palette:**
  - Takata Harness Green: `#007F3E` (RGB: 0, 127, 62)
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Accent Red (Dome logo): `#E60012`
  - Gunmetal / Black Aero: `#1A1A1A`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - The entire car is immersed in saturated Takata green.
  - A crisp white centerline arrow/stripe originates on the hood snout and flares outward over the roof scoop.
  - White Takata bold lowercase typography on doors; secondary sponsors (Philips, Mobil 1, BBS, Bridgestone).
  - Number 18 door placard.

### Pennzoil NISMO GT-R (R33, R34)
- **Concept:** High-contrast industrial predator. Bright solar yellow front clip transitioning into pitch-black lower rocker and rear deck.
- **Palette:**
  - Pennzoil Solar Yellow: `#FFCE00` (RGB: 255, 206, 0)
  - Satin Carbon Black: `#161616` (RGB: 22, 22, 22)
  - Pennzoil Bell Oval Red: `#DE2910`
  - White Typographic Hits: `#FFFFFF`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - The front bumper, hood, and roof are electric yellow. The lower side skirts and rear quarter haunches are solid black, visually dropping the center of gravity.
  - Red Pennzoil oval badge anchored centrally on doors and hood.
  - White NISMO and Bridgestone logos. Number 1 or 23.

### Raybrig Honda NSX (GT500)
- **Concept:** High-voltage nocturnal illumination. Raybrig (automotive lighting) represented by deep twilight metallic blue and crackling electric cyan lightning bolts.
- **Palette:**
  - Midnight Metallic Blue: `#0B1B3D`
  - Electric Cyan / Neon Teal: `#00D2FF`
  - Raybrig Crimson / Orange: `#FF3B30`
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - High-energy jagged lightning shards radiating from the headlights across the front fender flares into the doors.
  - Raybrig bold italic logotype on doors with orange accent bar.
  - Gold or bronze multi-spoke racing wheels. Number 100.

### HKS Oil Splash Nissan Skyline / Silvia
- **Concept:** Kinetic organic turbulence. Multi-colored fluid droplets and velocity specks erupting across a stealth black canvas.
- **Palette:**
  - Pure Gloss Black: `#0A0A0A`
  - HKS Magenta / Purple: `#8B1E8B`
  - HKS Teal / Mint: `#00BFA5`
  - HKS Racing Red: `#FF1744`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - Black background symbolizes raw mechanical power.
  - Fluid splashes and aerodynamic feathering stream from front canards and wheel wells across the flank.
  - Iconic HKS red/white block letters prominently positioned on doors and rear wing.

### JACCS Honda Accord (JTCC / JGTC)
- **Concept:** Confetti geometric mosaic. Playful yet ultra-sharp geometric triangles scattered across the car like celebratory track confetti.
- **Palette:**
  - Pure White Base: `#FFFFFF`
  - Emerald Green: `#009E49`
  - Signal Orange: `#FF6600`
  - Azure Blue: `#0072CE`
  - Primary Red: `#ED1C24`
- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - Triangles increase in density toward the rear of the car, creating visual forward acceleration.
  - Large JACCS title logo across doors with Michelin and Mugen technical support.

### PIAA Nakajima Racing Accord / NSX
- **Concept:** The Stormtrooper. Stripped-back, razor-sharp black and white minimalism.
- **Palette:**
  - Arctic White: `#F8F9FA`

- **Graphic Dynamics:**
  - Stark diagonal black wedges slicing across the rear quarter panel and C-pillar.
  - Giant "PIAA" block typography. Zero distracting secondary colors. Maximum raw contrast.

## 3. The Rules of Japanese Factory Racing Division Badging
Every manufacturer has an official skunkworks arm whose logo MUST be respected in authentic JGTC liveries:
- **Nissan:** NISMO (Nissan Motorsports) — red "O", crisp sans-serif. Always on hood snout, mirrors, and wing endplates.
- **Toyota:** TRD (Toyota Racing Development) or TOM'S (Tachi Oiwa Motor Sport).
- **Honda:** Mugen (無限 - "Unlimited") with the iconic three-color block (red, gold, black) or Honda Racing (HRC).
- **Subaru:** STI (Subaru Tecnica International) — cherry blossom red / magenta (*Sakura*) lettering.
- **Mazda:** Mazdaspeed — vibrant blue/orange or black/white racing heritage.
- **Mitsubishi:** Ralliart — slanted red and orange parallelogram bands.
