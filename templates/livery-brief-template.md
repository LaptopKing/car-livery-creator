Use this structured questionnaire to define and scope a new car livery project.

---

## 2. Style, School & Cultural Heritage
Select primary discipline:
- [ ] **JGTC / Super GT Golden Era:** High-speed corporate prestige, bold block sponsors, Japanese factory racing skunkworks (NISMO, Mugen, TRD, STI).
- [ ] **Japanese Street / Kanjozoku:** Osaka highway loop attitude, white door meatballs, racing nets, delivery parody livery (Yamato Transport, 7UP), bold Kanji scripts.
- [ ] **Kaido Racer / Bosozoku:** Super Silhouette block flares, long chin spoilers, rising sun rays, dual-tone candy colors, takeyari exhausts.
- [ ] **D1GP / Drift Culture:** Velocity shards, interlocking Falken scallops, neon highlighter accents, asymmetrical drift smoke splatter.
- [ ] **Retro-Futurism / Cyberpunk:** Synthwave neon, dark carbon fiber, Japanese Kanji/Katakana technical callouts, pixel gradient fades.
- [ ] **Character / Itasha Translation:** Translating anime or comic characters (Goku, Piccolo, Buzz Lightyear, Robin) into structural body panels.
- [ ] **Classic European / Le Mans Endurance:** Gulf center spear, Martini racing ribbons, Renown 787B argyle stitch, Apple Computer rainbow.
