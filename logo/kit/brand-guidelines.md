# Car Livery Creator — Brand & Logo Guidelines

This guide establishes the visual identity, color standards, clear space rules, and application protocols for the **Car Livery Creator** mark and typography.

---

## 1. The Core Mark: "The Vinyl Blade"

The mark represents the intersection of aerodynamic downforce and vehicle wrap craftsmanship:
- **The Forward Blade:** A 32° racing rake chevron in Electric High-Vis Speed Yellow (`#FFE600`).
- **The Trailing Flap:** An aerodynamic secondary canard in Crimson Velocity Red (`#FF1E42`).
- **The Knife Slash:** A precision horizontal negative-space slice symbolizing the plotter knife cut through multi-layered vinyl.
- **The Ground Effect Plate:** Arctic White (`#FFFFFF`) front dive-plane anchor.

## 3. Lockup Variants & File Directory

- `final-symbol.svg`: Official badge mark in dark squircle container.
- `final-symbol-mark.svg`: Transparent velocity blade mark (for application on car liveries and photos).
- `final-horizontal.svg`: Master horizontal lockup on dark background.
- `final-horizontal-transparent.svg`: Transparent horizontal lockup for light and dark backgrounds.
- `final-symbol-black.svg` / `final-symbol-white.svg`: One-color monochromatic masters.
- `final-symbol-app-icon-*.png`: PWA & mobile app icon masters (128, 512, 1024 px).
- `final-symbol-favicon-*.png`: High-clarity favicon suite.

---

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing

- **Clear Space:** Maintain an exclusion perimeter around the mark equal to at least **15% of the symbol width**. No competing text or logos may invade this boundary.
- **Minimum Digital Size:**
  - Mark only: `16 x 16 px` (retains full silhouette readability).
  - Full lockup: `180 px` width minimum.
- **Minimum Physical Size:**
  - Vinyl plot cutting: `25 mm` width minimum to ensure weedability of the knife slash.

---

## 5. Usage & Misuse Rules

- **DO** use the transparent mark (`final-symbol-mark.svg`) on vehicle fenders, rear wings, and windshield banners.
- **DO** maintain the 32° forward rake angle of the blades.
- **DO NOT** skew, stretch, or alter the aspect ratio of the blades.
- **DO NOT** replace the brand colors with uncalibrated random hues.
- **DO NOT** apply drop shadows or bevel filters—maintain pure, razor-sharp vector discipline.
